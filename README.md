# 🌱 FoodresQ

### Rescue Food. Recover Value. Reduce Waste.

**FoodresQ** is an AI-powered circular food recovery platform that connects **Restaurants, NGOs, Vendors, and Kirana Stores** to reduce food waste, recover financial value, and create measurable social impact.

---

## 🚀 What is FoodresQ?

Every day, perfectly usable food and near-expiry inventory can become waste.

FoodresQ creates two connected recovery ecosystems:

🍽️ **Restaurant Surplus → NGO → People in Need**

🏪 **Kirana Near-Expiry Inventory → Vendor → Value Recovery**

Our platform uses **AI-powered insights, smart matching, trust scoring, and analytics** to make food recovery faster, smarter, and more transparent.

---

## 👥 Platform Roles

### 🍽️ Restaurant

Restaurants can:

- 📦 Post surplus food
- 🤝 Donate surplus food to NGOs
- 📊 Track food recovery
- 📈 View waste and recovery analytics
- 🤖 Get AI-powered surplus predictions

---

### 🤝 NGO

NGOs can:

- 🔎 Discover available surplus food
- ⚡ Accept rescue opportunities
- ⏱️ Respond within an 8-minute acceptance window
- 🧠 Get smart NGO matching
- 🚚 Manage pickups and distributions
- ⭐ Build an NGO Trust Score
- 🍱 Track meals saved and people served
- 📊 Monitor social impact

---

### 🛒 Vendor

Vendors can:

- 🔎 Discover discounted near-expiry products
- 💰 Purchase products at reduced prices
- 🏷️ Find attractive deals
- 📦 Track purchases
- 💵 Monitor total savings

---

### 🏪 Kirana Store

Kirana stores can:

- 📦 Manage inventory
- ⏳ Identify near-expiry products
- 🤖 Get AI expiry-risk insights
- 🏷️ Create discounted offers
- 🤝 Connect with Vendors
- 💰 Recover revenue from at-risk inventory
- 📊 Track sales and inventory performance
- 🌱 Monitor waste prevention

## ⭐ NGO Trust Score

FoodresQ uses a **Trust Score** to support reliable food-rescue matching.

The score can consider:

- Successful collections

- On-time pickups

- Distribution completion

- Acceptance reliability

- Completion consistency

- Verified impact

This helps create a more reliable food recovery network.
